Our story

Born on the touchline.

Stackly started in 2019, on the sideline of a community league final that nearly fell apart over a rescheduled fixture and three unread group chats.

We realized the game itself was never the problem — the paper around it was. So we built one quiet place where tournaments, teams, schedules, venues, and live scores finally agree with each other.

  • Founded by league organizers who were tired of spreadsheet archaeology.
  • Designed alongside 120+ clubs, schools, and community tournaments.
  • Remote-first team, present in every time zone we support.
